In 2012, I made the decision to begin hormone replacement therapy. This game is about the next six months of my life, made up of playable vignettes I created at a rate of one or two a day.

This Journal Game is not meant as a definitive guide to the process of HRT. It is an attempt at documenting the emotional experience of transitioning in the 2010s, created for other trans people who might have related.

**FLASHING WARNING: This game contains "glitchy" color-flashing effects during its opening, on its menu screen, and during transitions between vignettes.**

CONTENT WARNING: This game deals with transphobia and transmisogyny, dysphoria, and the perils of medical transition (including having doctors deny care and buying medications from gray market sellers).

ALSO NOTE: This game is built in Flash, and the Mac version may no longer run on new computers.
