[*Grand Theft Auto VI*](https://www.keengamer.com/games/grand-theft-auto-vi/) launches on **November 19, 2026**, and the wait has turned into one of the longest marketing runs the industry has seen. Rockstar confirmed the game in February 2022, so the build-up has run for more than four and a half years.

<video src="https://cdn.jwplayer.com/manifests/wDWgnXd4.m3u8?max_resolution=1280" controls=""></video>

Two older launches give that run some useful context. *Grand Theft Auto: Vice City* in 2002 and *Grand Theft Auto V* in 2013 both arrived as the **biggest games of their years**, but the hype and the release calendars looked very different. Comparing all three shows how much a GTA launch now bends the schedule for everyone else.

## 1\. How the Hype Compares Across Vice City, GTA V, and GTA VI

![YouTube preview](https://www.keengamer.com/wp-content/uploads/videos/2026_11/youtube-thumb-rz1B4B2yBZY-small.webp)

The fastest way to see the change is the gap between announcement and launch. *Vice City* went from reveal to store shelves in **160 days**. *GTA V* took **693 days**. *GTA VI* will reach **1,749 days** on launch day, more than ten times the *Vice City* campaign.

The table below lines up the key milestones for each game, from the first announcement to the headline record each one set before or at launch.

| Milestone | Vice City | GTA V | GTA VI |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Announced** | May 22, 2002 | Oct 25, 2011 | Feb 4, 2022 |
| **Launch date** | Oct 29, 2002 | Sept 17, 2013 | Nov 19, 2026 |
| **Days to launch** | 160 | 693 | 1,749 |
| **Platforms** | PS2 | PS3, Xbox 360 | PS5, Xbox Series X\|S |
| **Headline record** | 2002’s top seller | $1B in 3 days | PS/Xbox pre-order record |

The records column tells its own story. *Vice City* and *GTA V* earned their headlines with sales after launch. *GTA VI* set its records before it went on sale, first with a **475-million-view trailer** and then as the most pre-ordered game in PlayStation Store and Microsoft Store history.

## 2\. GTA Vice City’s 2002 Launch and the Games Around It

[![Image showing neon-lit Ocean Drive at night with the Collonn Hotel, the Moonlite sign, and a yellow taxi in Grand Theft Auto: Vice City The Definitive Edition](https://www.keengamer.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/GTA-Vice-City-780x439.webp)](https://images.keengamer.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/GTA-Vice-City.jpg)

GTA Vice City

The *Vice City* hype cycle was short and loud. Rockstar revealed the game at **E3 on May 22, 2002**, and [Take-Two announced it had shipped](https://ir.take2games.com/static-files/63b50ea9-bc60-4cc8-9cb6-1b7a3718507b) to North American stores on **October 29, 2002**, with Europe following on November 8. There was no multi-year teaser campaign, because *Grand Theft Auto III* had only launched a year earlier.

Rockstar went into launch week confident. In the shipping announcement, Rockstar Games president **Sam Houser** measured the new game directly against the one that had just reshaped the series.

> “Grand Theft Auto 3 set the bar incredibly high but we are confident that Grand Theft Auto: Vice City dramatically exceeds the standards we set last year.”

That short gap shaped the hype. Players were still deep in *GTA III*, which finished 2002 as the **third best-selling game** in the US. *Vice City* landed on top of that momentum and became the year’s number one seller, with **4,446,676 copies** sold in the US in 2002 alone.

### The Fall 2002 Release Calendar

[![Image showing Tommy Vercetti holding a pistol in front of the glowing Ocean View hotel sign in Grand Theft Auto: Vice City The Definitive Edition](https://www.keengamer.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Tommy-Vercetti-780x439.webp)](https://images.keengamer.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Tommy-Vercetti.jpg)

Tommy Vercetti

The 2002 holiday season did not clear a path for Rockstar. Big releases landed right next to *Vice City*, and none of them moved to avoid it.

- *Kingdom Hearts*: September 17, 2002, six weeks before *Vice City*.
- *Sly Cooper and the Thievius Raccoonus*: September 23, 2002.
- *Hitman 2: Silent Assassin*: October 1, 2002.
- *Tony Hawk’s Pro Skater 4*: October 23, 2002, six days before *Vice City*.
- *Ratchet & Clank*: November 4, 2002, six days after *Vice City*.

Those games still found big audiences. *Tony Hawk’s Pro Skater 4* sold **1,510,226 copies** in the US that year, and *Kingdom Hearts* passed one million in the US. In 2002, a GTA launch was the biggest game of the season, but it shared the shelf with everyone else.

## 3\. GTA V’s 2013 Launch and the Games Around It

[![Image showing the downtown Los Santos skyline at dusk above a wet freeway with a police helicopter overhead in Grand Theft Auto V](https://www.keengamer.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/GTA-V-780x439.webp)](https://images.keengamer.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/GTA-V.jpg)

GTA V

*Grand Theft Auto V* brought the first true long-form GTA hype campaign. Rockstar announced the game on **October 25, 2011**, released the first trailer on November 2, and then set a spring 2013 window. On **January 31, 2013**, Rockstar moved the launch to September 17, 2013, citing extra development time, and paired the four-month delay with an apology to fans.

> “To all Grand Theft Auto fans, please accept our apologies for the delay, and our promise that the entire team here is working very hard to make the game all it can be.”

The wait paid off at the register. *GTA V* sold **11.21 million copies** and made **$815.7 million** in its first 24 hours. [Guinness World Records lists it](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/108152-fastest-videogame-to-gross-1-billion) as the fastest entertainment property of any kind to reach **$1 billion**, a mark it hit in three days. *Call of Duty: Black Ops II*, the previous record holder, needed 15 days.

### The Fall 2013 Release Calendar

[![Image showing Trevor Philips carrying a jerry can away from a burning truck by the Sunset Shores motel sign in Grand Theft Auto V](https://www.keengamer.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Trevor-Philips-780x439.webp)](https://images.keengamer.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Trevor-Philips.jpg)

Trevor Philips

The September date put *GTA V* in a two-month pocket before the busiest stretch of 2013. The PS4 and Xbox One launched that November, and most publishers built their fall plans around the new consoles.

- *Saints Row IV*: August 20, 2013, four weeks before *GTA V*.
- *FIFA 14*: September 24, 2013, one week after *GTA V*.
- *Batman: Arkham Origins*: October 25, 2013.
- *Battlefield 4* and *Assassin’s Creed IV: Black Flag*: October 29, 2013.
- *Call of Duty: Ghosts*: November 5, 2013, 49 days after *GTA V*.
- **PlayStation 4 and Xbox One:** November 15 and November 22, 2013.

Even with that record start, *GTA V* did not scare the calendar away. *Call of Duty: Ghosts* also reached **$1 billion** in 2013, launching seven weeks later. The fall schedule moved for new hardware, and *GTA V* simply owned the weeks before it.

## 4\. GTA VI’s Hype Before the November 19 Launch

[![Image showing the Vice City skyline with a highway bridge, a crowded beach, and boats on the bay in Grand Theft Auto VI](https://www.keengamer.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Leonida-780x439.webp)](https://images.keengamer.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Leonida.jpg)

Leonida

The *GTA VI* campaign is longer, bigger, and more public than anything before it, with **two delays** along the way. Rockstar [confirmed development on February 4, 2022](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/ak73k92o47ko75/grand-theft-auto-community-update), at the bottom of a *GTA Online* community update. The first trailer arrived 22 months later, and the road from there included a Netflix premiere, several leaks, and a pre-order record.

### Trailers and Viewership Records

Rockstar planned to release the first trailer on December 5, 2023, but [posted it early after a leak](https://www.keengamer.com/articles/news/grand-theft-auto-vi-trailer-released-early-after-leak-launching-in-2025/). It reached **93 million YouTube views** in 24 hours. The [second trailer](https://www.keengamer.com/articles/news/gta-vi-now-meet-lucia-and-jason-with-the-new-trailer/) dropped without warning on **May 6, 2025**, and Rockstar [called it the biggest video launch of all time](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/at-nearly-half-a-billion-views-rockstar-says-gta-6s-new-trailer-is-the-biggest-video-launch-of-all-time), with **475 million views** across all platforms in its first day.

The **Extended Look** took the hype outside gaming entirely. The 27-minute gameplay showcase premiered on Netflix on **August 27, 2026**, and Netflix counted **31.1 million views** in its first four days, topping its charts in 87 of 93 countries. The [GTA 6 Extended Look recap](https://www.keengamer.com/articles/news/gta-6-extended-look-recap-all-gameplay-details/) covers every gameplay detail it showed.

### Delays and Leaks Before Launch

[![Image showing Jason leaning on a red sports car next to Lucia in front of stacked shipping containers in Grand Theft Auto VI](https://www.keengamer.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Jason-and-Lucia-770x439.webp)](https://images.keengamer.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Jason-and-Lucia.jpg)

Jason and Lucia

*GTA VI* was first set for **fall 2025**. On May 2, 2025, Rockstar moved it to May 26, 2026, and on November 6, 2025, it [announced the November 19, 2026 date](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/ak3ak31a49a221/grand-theft-auto-vi-is-now-set-to-launch-november-19-2026) with an apology that echoed the *GTA V* message from 12 years earlier.

> “We are sorry for adding additional time to what we realize has been a long wait, but these extra months will allow us to finish the game with the level of polish you have come to expect and deserve.”

Leaks became part of the story too. A wave of **leaked footage** spread in August 2026, and Rockstar called the experience heartbreaking for its team in an official statement. The October [CyberLeek ending clips leak](https://www.keengamer.com/articles/news/cyberleek-leaks-gta-vi-ending-clips-in-new-video/) added more pressure. Neither moved the date, and the [GTA VI launch trailer](https://www.keengamer.com/articles/news/gta-vi-launch-trailer-is-out-now-shows-more-of-jason-and-lucia/) confirmed November 19 again.

### Pre-Order Records and Pre-Load

[![Vice City in GTA 6](https://www.keengamer.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Vice-City-780x439.webp)](https://www.keengamer.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Vice-City-scaled.jpg)

Vice City in GTA 6

Pre-orders opened on **June 25, 2026**, at **$79.99** for the Standard Edition and **$99.99** for the Ultimate Edition. The [GTA 6 prices and editions guide](https://www.keengamer.com/articles/guides/gta-6-prices-and-editions-which-edition-should-you-buy/) breaks down both.

Four and a half months later, those pre-orders broke records on both console storefronts. On **November 10**, Sony confirmed *GTA VI* as the [most pre-ordered game](https://www.keengamer.com/articles/news/gta-6-reportedly-becomes-the-most-pre-ordered-game-of-all-time-on-both-ps5-and-xbox/) in PlayStation Store history, and Microsoft confirmed the same record for the Microsoft Store. The [PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/10/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-on-ps5-starting-today/) tied the record directly to the early pre-load.

> “Given this unprecedented demand, we’ve joined with Rockstar Games to allow players to start pre-loading the game nine days early.”

Take-Two struck the same tone a day earlier. In its [November 9 earnings release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000946581/000162828026072175/ttwo2q27earningsrelease.htm), CEO **Strauss Zelnick** held the launch date and the company’s **$8 to $8.2 billion** net bookings outlook for fiscal 2027.

> “The launch of Grand Theft Auto VI is ten days away and, with pre-orders continuing to reach unprecedented levels, we expect to deliver an historic year for our company.”

[GTA VI pre-load went live](https://www.keengamer.com/articles/news/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-load-is-now-live-for-all-digital-pre-orders/) for all digital pre-orders on November 10, with a download of up to **212 GB**. Boxed editions reached stores on November 12 with a [download code instead of a disc](https://www.keengamer.com/articles/news/gta-6-physical-edition-and-double-sided-poster-revealed-ahead-of-launch/). The [GTA 6 preload and global release times guide](https://www.keengamer.com/articles/guides/gta-6-preload-date-and-global-release-times-guide/) lists the unlock time for every region.

## 5\. Games That Moved Out of GTA VI’s Release Window

[![Image showing a pilot walking toward a parked fighter ship with an open canopy in a hangar at night in Squadron 42](https://www.keengamer.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Squadron-42-780x439.webp)](https://images.keengamer.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Squadron-42.jpg)

Squadron 42

This is the clearest break from 2002 and 2013. *Vice City* and *GTA V* shared their seasons with other big games. *GTA VI* has publishers openly stepping aside, and some of them have said so in their own announcements.

- *Squadron 42*: Cloud Imperium moved the game from **Q4 2026 to Q2 2027** in an August 27, 2026 Letter from the Chairman. Chris Roberts [named GTA VI directly](https://pcgamer.com/games/fps/star-citizens-squadron-42-delayed-again-this-time-to-avoid-the-attention-buzz-saw-of-gta-6-says-chris-roberts) as the reason.
- *Fable*: Xbox moved Playground Games’ reboot out of its **autumn 2026** window in May 2026, saying the move gives the game “the dedicated moment it deserves.” At the June 7 Xbox Games Showcase, it [set February 23, 2027](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/after-being-delayed-to-2027-fable-finally-has-an-actual-release-date/) as the new date.

Roberts put the reasoning more bluntly than any publisher did in 2002 or 2013. His letter ties the delay to the coverage GTA VI will pull from every outlet and creator.

> “But much like the rest of the industry there is no way I want to launch my spiritual successor to Wing Commander … launching into the attention buzz saw of GTA 6.”

[![Image showing a rider on a brown horse looking toward a white castle with tall spires in Fable](https://www.keengamer.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Fable-780x439.webp)](https://images.keengamer.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Fable.jpg)

Fable

The games that stayed in fall 2026 mostly launched early. *Gears of War: E-Day* arrived on **October 6**, and *Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4* reached its full launch on **October 23**, both weeks ahead of Rockstar.

The biggest November name, *The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time* Remake, arrived on Switch 2 on **November 5**, two weeks before *GTA VI*, and the [best games releasing in November 2026](https://www.keengamer.com/articles/features/monthly-highlights/top-8-best-games-releasing-in-november-2026/) list has nothing else of that size near launch week. In 2013, *Call of Duty* and two new consoles launched in the weeks after *GTA V*. In 2026, the biggest fall releases are clearing out before Rockstar arrives.

The 2026 shift also has a pre-launch side. *GTA V* leaves the **PS Plus Game Catalog** on November 17, two days before launch, and Sony’s [PS Plus notice for GTA 5](https://www.keengamer.com/articles/news/gta-5-is-available-on-ps-plus-for-a-limited-time-before-gta-6-launches/) pairs the departure with a *GTA VI* pre-order link, so even the older game now works as marketing for the new one.

## 6\. What Vice City and GTA V Say About GTA VI’s Launch

[![Image showing Raul Bautista in a white suit checking his watch with a leather duffel bag on his shoulder in Grand Theft Auto VI](https://www.keengamer.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Raul-Batista-780x439.webp)](https://images.keengamer.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Raul-Batista-scaled.jpg)

Raul Batista

Put side by side, the three launches show GTA hype changing **shape as well as size**. Back in 2002, hype meant a strong E3 reveal and a five-month sprint.

In 2013, it meant a two-year trailer campaign that ended in a sales record. In 2026, the hype itself is the event, with trailers outdrawing movie premieres and a gameplay showcase topping Netflix.

The release calendar tells the same story. *Tony Hawk’s Pro Skater 4* and *Ratchet & Clank* launched within a week of *Vice City*. *FIFA 14* and *Call of Duty: Ghosts* launched within seven weeks of *GTA V*. In 2026, studios are **rewriting their schedules** and saying the reason out loud.

That leaves *GTA VI* with something neither predecessor had: a launch window that the rest of the industry has cleared on purpose, and a pre-order record already in hand.

The next record to watch is the one that started the comparison, *GTA V* ‘s **$1 billion in three days**, once *GTA VI* launches on November 19.
