## You Have Reached Vi åker jeep!

### Posted on May 1st, 2007 by Jeepen

![](https://jeepen.org/images/img14.jpg)

*You have arrived. You came by jeep.*

Welcome to the home of [jeepform](https://jeepen.org/dict) on the web, the Vi åker jeep homepage.

[Vi åker jeep \[Eng.: We go by jeep\]](https://jeepen.org/name.html) is a group of scandinavian role-players and game wrights pushing their style of play, known as [jeepform](https://jeepen.org/dict) to the rest of the civilised role-playing world.

A list of upcoming games is available [here](https://jeepen.org/games/upcoming.html).

## The Jeep, it is A-changing

### Posted on April 16th, 2008 by Jeepen

2007 saw the inauguration of two new jeeps: [Anna Westerling!](https://jeepen.org/jeepers/index.html#anna) and [Anna-Karin Linder](https://jeepen.org/jeepers/index.html#anna-karin). Anna is a producer genious with a nick for larp and theatre and led the production and creative vision of [A Nice Evening with the Family](http://enstillamiddag.se/). Anna-Karin studies theatre and created the larp The Last Supper (with Kristoffer Lind), and recently held a [meta techniques](https://jeepen.org/dict/solmukohta08.html) workshop at [Solmukohta 2008](http://www.solmukohta.org/) for a full room.

Already in 2008, we have inaugurated another two new jeeps: [Ulrik Lehrskov-Schmidt](https://jeepen.org/jeepers/index.html#ulrik) and [J. Tuomas Harviainen](https://jeepen.org/jeepers/index.html#jtuomas). Ulrik is one of the master-minds behind the Fastaval Café, the place to be at [Fastaval](http://fastaval.dk/) and has single-handedly made reality of the upcoming JeepCon in July. We all wish we would have had him amongst us for years and years. Finally, J. Tuomas is the second Finnish addition to the jeep crew. He is a frequent and well-played larpwright, single father, librarian that knows a thing or two about Japanese rope bondage. He is also the first non-jeeper to have a [game](http://jeepen.org/games/aftertaste/) hosted on the jeep site.

We are also going to inaugurate [Martin Jordö](https://jeepen.org/jeepers/index.html#martinj) and [Daniel Wilhelm-Olsson](https://jeepen.org/jeepers/index.html#dante) as soon as we can meet up with them somewhere and share a schnapps.

Frederik remarked that if we can team Ulrik up with Anna, they will be unstoppable.
