**Vivian James** is in essence what happens when you combine [Memetic Mutation](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Memetic_Mutation "Memetic Mutation"), [Image Boards](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Image_Boards "Image Boards"), video games and controversy into one character. Although she's inevitably linked with [Gamergate](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Gamergate "Gamergate"), her origins can be [traced back to 2014](http://knowyourmeme.com/memes/vivian-james), when [4chan](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/4chan "4chan") 's /v/ board and indie developer group The Fine Young Capitalists collaborated to design a character for a game jam (later revealed to be for the indie game *[Afterlife Empire](http://store.steampowered.com/app/367710/)*) in the wake of the image board's donations. Since then however, she's taken a life all of her own.

Generally depicted as a red-haired, freckled female gamer wearing jeans and a striped sweater, Vivian at first glance seems like an unlikely figure to attract so much attention ([for good or ill](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Flame_War "Flame War")). Still, she's seen as many things. Most notably, a symbol of [Gamergate](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Gamergate "Gamergate"). But she's also an embodiment of both [4chan](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/4chan "4chan") and [8chan](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/8chan "8chan") 's /v/ boards. A representative of modern gamers - and gaming at large - as they are, rather than what certain elements wish them to be. A legitimate gamer girl who's not afraid to be who she is or fight for what she believes in, be it ethics in games journalism or video games in general.

In addition to Vivian's originally intended game, she has appeared in various image albums, fan/webcomics [^1] and even cosplay. [Over time](https://www.reddit.com/r/KotakuInAction/comments/4c1num/misc_an_introduction_to_vivian_james_her_family/), she's also been accompanied by [a growing, colorful cast of characters](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Loads_and_Loads_of_Characters "Loads and Loads of Characters"). And is slated to be a major character in an upcoming game called *[Project SOCJUS](http://anomalousgames.tumblr.com/)* and a playable character in a [Game Mod](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Game_Mod "Game Mod") -in-progress for *[XCOM 2](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/X-COM_\(Video_Game\) "X-COM (Video Game)")*.[^2]

## Vivian James provides examples of the following tropes:

- [A Girl and Her Sea Lion](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/A_Boy_and_His_X "A Boy and His X"): How her and Ethics are oft portrayed.
- [Anthropomorphic Personification](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Anthropomorphic_Personification "Anthropomorphic Personification"): After a fashion. Aside from being associated with the /v/ boards in both [4chan](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/4chan "4chan") and [8chan](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/8chan "8chan"), she's sometimes affectionately used to symbolize modern gaming. And sometimes used as a [Straw Character](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Straw_Character "Straw Character") by anti-GG, [corrupting](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Wimpification "Wimpification") her established [carefree personality](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Stoic "The Stoic").
	- The original personification of /v/ is usually shown as Vivian's father as well.
		- Some particularly "safe space"-y cons even banned ' [all “vivian James” cosplay, no matter how subtle](http://dashcon--2015.tumblr.com/post/120153572051/rules-and-regulations-how-to-have-a-safe) '. How one can reliably tell "subtle" Vivian James cosplay from [Gwendolyn Tennyson](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Ben_10 "Ben 10") cosplay or a natural redhead in a sweater remains a mystery.
- [Art Shift](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Art_Shift "Art Shift"): The art styles used for her and others varies considerably, ranging from Macha-inspired portraits to photorealistic to [Animesque](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Animesque "Animesque").
- [Ascended Meme](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Ascended_Meme "Ascended Meme"):
	- Vivian's outfit [was given a nod](http://imgur.com/yfmADmy) in the anime *Rakudai Kishi no Cavalry* in 2015.
		- In addition to having taken a life of her own, Vivian even has [a film award](http://thefineyoungcapitalists.tumblr.com/post/149345947545/happy-birthday-vivian-james) named in her honor by The Fine Young Capitalists in 2016, to coincide with her "birthday" and indirectly with Gamergate's second year anniversary.
		- She's grown popular enough to have [attracted](https://twitter.com/kosan_takeuchi/status/789110884501041152?lang=en) [fanart](https://twitter.com/kosan_takeuchi/status/842990130981871617?lang=en) from Japanese artist and game designer Ko Takeuchi.
- [Badass](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Badass "Badass"): Sometimes portrayed as one, often of the [Action Girl](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Action_Girl "Action Girl") variety.
- [The Bechdel Test](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Bechdel_Test "The Bechdel Test"): Not only do Vivian and pals mock the test, but they even pass it.
- [Berserk Button](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Berserk_Button "Berserk Button"): For Vivian, it's anything that slanders gamers, detrimental to gaming and otherwise patronizing to women like her. [Also](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Arson,_Murder,_and_Jaywalking "Arson, Murder, and Jaywalking"), [scrubs](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Scrub "Scrub") and "filthy [casuals](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Casual_Video_Game "Casual Video Game")."
	- She's also this in real life to certain (primarily anti-GG) individuals and groups, who view her as offensive, misogynistic and reactionary.
- [Best Her to Bed Her](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Best_Her_to_Bed_Her "Best Her to Bed Her"): Vivian is sometimes portrayed as this even in NSFW works, stemming from her rather competitive nature and hardcore gamer streak.
- [Big Name Fan](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Big_Name_Fan "Big Name Fan"):
	- While not exactly "big," [KiteTales](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/KiteTales "KiteTales") is slated to voice Vivian in *Project SOCJUS*.
		- Ko Takeuchi of [Nintendo](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Nintendo "Nintendo") fame has also made fanart of her.

- [Breakout Character](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Breakout_Character "Breakout Character"): Originally intended for the indie game *Afterlife Empire*, Vivian has since taken on a life of her own, along a number of other personified characters. To the point that [cosplay exists](http://imgur.com/a/GRknl) based on her.

		- Not Vivian per se, but her outfit does pop up [in an episode](http://imgur.com/yfmADmy) of the anime *Radukai Kishi no Cavalry.*

- [Godwin's Law](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Godwin%27s_Law "Godwin's Law"): "Uncle" /pol/ and his daughter, Erika Polina, who both look like Nazis in all but name, which is a nod to /pol/'s irreverent notoriety. Erika herself was originally a parody of Lillian Woods with the [unintentionally Aryan elements](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Unfortunate_Implications "Unfortunate Implications") taken [Up to Eleven](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Up_to_Eleven "Up to Eleven") to the point of even dressing like she walked straight out of the [League of German Girls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/League_of_German_Girls).[^6]
- [Hair Decorations](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Hair_Decorations "Hair Decorations"): A rather unusual example. Vivian's headband is actually there to identify either her origins on [4chan](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/4chan "4chan") or her transplanting to [8chan](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/8chan "8chan"), depending on whether the hair dec in question depicts a 4 leaf clover or an infinity symbol respectively.
- [Hidden Depths](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Hidden_Depths "Hidden Depths"):
	- Vivian's usually shown to be rather knowledgeable, despite seemingly disinterested at times on anything not related to playing video games.
		- Gilda Mars, despite her ditziness and [Pollyanna tendencies](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Pollyanna "The Pollyanna"), isn't afraid to speak her mind or use her upvote/downvote judgements when push comes to shove.[^7]
		- Lillian shows occasional signs of self-awareness regarding her hypocrisy and views, in addition to how even she has standards, especially with regard to the likes of Gawker.

- [Insult Backfire](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Insult_Backfire "Insult Backfire"):
	- Lillian Woods was originally created as a Vivian James knock-off by anti-GG to both mock [Gamergate](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Gamergate "Gamergate") supporters and create a "proper" representation of female gamers. Not only did it ironically result in Lillian looking very much like a [veritable Aryan girl, blond hair, blue eyes and all](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Unfortunate_Implications "Unfortunate Implications"), but she was ultimately embraced by pro-GG as both Vivian's "rival" and a caricature of anti-GG.
		- Ethics, the Sea Lion is a direct result of this as well, being a [Take That](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Take_That "Take That") to accusations of "sealioning."
		- Attempts by various individuals, including publications like *Vice* to frame her as [an anti-feminist, reactionary symbol](https://archive.today/20150824001144/http://www.vice.com/read/meet-the-female-gamer-mascot-created-by-anti-feminists-828) have done little more than serve as unintentional promotion for her.

- [The Pollyanna](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Pollyanna "The Pollyanna"): Gilda Mars tends to show signs of this, her obliviousness at times reflecting the disconnect between [Reddit](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Reddit "Reddit") 's users and its mods as well as the ones running the site itself.
- [Punny Name](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Punny_Name "Punny Name"): Vivian James is a play on "Video Games." While Lillian Woods is a play on the term "Literally Who."
- [Reality Subtext](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Reality_Subtext "Reality Subtext"): Depending on the artist, the hair dec Vivian wears is either the clover associated with [4chan](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/4chan "4chan") or the infinity logo of [8chan](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/8chan "8chan"). Which stems from schisms in [4chan](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/4chan "4chan") over censorship and a subsequent migration of certain anons to [8chan](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/8chan "8chan").

		- "Punished /v/'s" appearance is not only a direct reference to *[Metal Gear Solid V: The Phantom Pain](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Metal_Gear_Solid_V:_The_Phantom_Pain "Metal Gear Solid V: The Phantom Pain")* but also a nod to 8chan's /v/ board being founded by anons from 4chan's equivalent.

Since then however, she's gone well beyond that.
