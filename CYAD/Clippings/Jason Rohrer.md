| "Writing is a kind of magic or deep-frozen speech, which the writer can use, day after day, to say to everyone who looks at it whatever he wants to say." -- John Holt, *Atlantic Monthly*, 1971  ---  Currently parenting and making video games somewhere in New Hampshire.      \[[worklog](http://northcountrynotes.org/jason-rohrer/worklog)\] \[[release log](https://hcsoftware.sourceforge.net/jason-rohrer/releaseLog.html)\] \[[support my work](https://hcsoftware.sourceforge.net/jason-rohrer/supportMyWork.php)\]      Want to know when I release something?   You'll get a few brief emails a year, at most.       ## Real-World Games  [Project Skydrop](https://projectskydrop.com/) -- a real-world treasure hunt in a shrinking circle. Ended October 2, 2024.      [A Game for Someone](https://www.polygon.com/2013/3/28/4157884/game-designer-jason-rohrer-designs-a-game-meant-to-be-played-2000) \[[video](https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1017715/Humanity-s-Last-Game-The)\]-- a titanium board game that won't be played by anyone for at least 2000 years. Available now, somewhere in the Nevada Desert.       ## Artificial Intelligence Projects  [Project December](http://projectdecember.net/) -- The first seemingly-sentient conversational AI, more than 2 years before ChatGPT existed.       ## Video Games  [One Hour One Life](http://onehouronelife.com/) -- my twelfth game. A multiplayer survival game of parenting and civilization building. Available now.      [Cordial Minuet](http://cordialminuet.com/) -- my eleventh game. A two-player online strategy game played for real money.      [The Castle Doctrine](http://thecastledoctrine.net/) -- my tenth game. A massively-multiplayer game of burglary and home defense.      [Diamond Trust of London](http://diamondtrustgame.com/) -- my ninth game. A two-player strategy game about deception.      [Inside a Star-filled Sky](http://insideastarfilledsky.net/) -- my eighth game. An infinite, recursive, tactical shooter for one player.      [Sleep Is Death](http://sleepisdeath.net/) -- my seventh game. A two-player storytelling game.      [Primrose](http://primrose.sourceforge.net/) -- my sixth game. A compelling tile-placement puzzle game. Designed with the iPhone in mind.      [Between](https://hcsoftware.sourceforge.net/between/) -- my fifth game, about consciousness and isolation. Hosted by *Esquire* magazine as part of their December 2008 Best and Brightest feature (see [my profile](http://www.esquire.com/features/best-and-brightest-2008/future-of-video-game-design-1208) in *Esquire*).      [Gravitation](http://hcsoftware.sourceforge.net/gravitation) -- my fourth game, about mania, melancholia, and the creative process.      [Passage](http://hcsoftware.sourceforge.net/passage) -- my third game, shown at Kokoromi's curated [GAMMA 256](http://www.kokoromi.org/projects/gamma256) event (at [MIGS](http://www.sijm.ca/en) 2007).      [Cultivation](http://cultivation.sourceforge.net/) -- my second game, a social simulation about a community of gardeners that explores the balance between conflict and compromise (no shooting, but plenty of angry looks).      [Transcend](http://transcend.sourceforge.net/) -- my first game, an abstract 2D shooting game that doubles as a multimedia sculpture.
