# The Logic of Digital Gaming.
## Abstract
> The article presents the author's analysis of video games in terms of appearance and behavior. His analysis shows how video games effectively create and eliminate disjuncture with the gamer's experience. The author suggests that violence in the game may become acerbated in an effort to shock players back into reality and remind them that there is a real world out there. He challenges the readers to address the possibility of the elimination of the user enhanced through user interaction itself.
