#### 01. Play is

En esta primera parte, el hablante introduce el tema del libro y su relación con el juego, destacando la importancia que se le otorga al juego, incluso comparándolo con la cultura. Se menciona que el juego es contextual, apropiativo, carnivalesco, disruptivo, autotélico, creativo y personal. Se discute cómo el juego se apropia del espacio y de las personas, utilizando ejemplos como el videojuego Grand Theft Auto para ilustrar el carácter carnivalesco y la naturaleza disruptiva de ciertas experiencias de juego. Además, se señala la diferencia entre el juego y el "playfulness", indicando que el primero tiene un fin o un objetivo, mientras que el segundo no necesariamente lo tiene. En resumen, se enfatiza la expresión personal y la creatividad que surgen a través del juego.

(Tema::Lo que es el juego (12))
	Es una forma de entender lo que nos rodea. Para el autor es una forma de ser humano, en lo que se puede considerar casi todo. Su pensamiento abarca un poco más que [[Huizinga, J. (2007). Homo Ludens.]]. Alguncas citas importantes son:
	(12)
		To play is to be in the world. Playing is a form of understanding what surrounds us and who we are, and a way of engaging with others. Play is a mode of being human.
	(13) Como manifestación y estar en el mundo
		Play is a manifestation of humanity, used for expressing and being in the world.
	(15) El juego no importa tanto, sino el jugar
		Games don’t matter that much. They are a manifestation, a form of and for play, just not the only one. 
	(16) Sobre lo moral en el juego y del porqué jugamos
		play is a fundamental part of our moral well-being, of the healthy and mature and complete human life. Through play we experience the world, we construct it and we destroy it, and we explore who we are and what we can say. Play frees us from moral conventions but makes them still present, so we are aware of their weight, presence, and importance. We need play precisely because we need occasional freedom and distance from our conventional understanding of the moral fabric of society.
	(30) Quienes somos en el mundo
		Play is finding expression; it is letting us understand the world and, through that understanding, challenging the establishment, leading for knowledge, and creating new ties or breaking old ones. But ultimately whatever we do in play stays with us. Play is a singularly individual experience—shared, yes, but meaningful only in the way it scaffolds an individual experience of the world. Through play, we are in the world

(Tema::Orden y caos en el jugar (14))
	Hay orden y destrucción en el juego.
		Play is always on the verge of destruction, of itself and of its players, and that is precisely why it matters. Play is a movement between order and chaos.
	(20) Entre la creación y la destrucción
		Play is between the rational pleasures of order and creation and the sweeping euphoria of destruction and rebirth, between the Apollonian and the Dionysiac
		

(Tema::Sobre el dark play (14))
		Dark play, with its potential dangers and exhilarating results, is another example of the nature of play as a way of being in the world—a dangerous one.
	(26) Como las actividades o juegos del festejo en el mundial
		The disruptive nature of play allows us to understand the perils of play as well. By disrupting the context in which it takes place, play is a creative, expressive force. But this force has its dangers too. Dark play is an exploration of the wild side of play in which players decide to engage in an activity, like Camover, to force an emotional response in those who do not recognize they are actually playing.

(Tema::Sobre lo carnivalesco en el juego (14))
	Por la forma en que subvierte una situación 
		Play appropriates events, structures, and institutions to mock them and trivialize them, or make them deadly serious. The carnival of the Middle Ages, with its capacity to subvert conventions and institutions in a suspension of time and power,13 was a symptom of freedom
	(15) Como forma de expresión
		Through carnivalesque play, we express ourselves, taking over the world to laugh at it and make sense of it too.

(Tema::El juego es contextual (17))
	El juego es contextual, determinado por un contexto.
		Context comprises the environment in which we play, the technologies with which we play, and the potential companions of play.
 
(Tema::Contexto (16))
	Contexto es la unificación de la acción y el significado; es la forma en en cómo las acciones pueden tener un significado.

(Tema::El diseño en el juego (18))
	Se diseña para facilitar la emergencia del juego

(Tema::Lo que son las reglas (19))
	Permiten la creación e identificación de un contexto
		Rules are the formal instruments that allow the creation and shared identification of a context of play. All contexts of play have rules of some type.

(Tema::El jugar como afirmación de nuestra humanidad (20))
	Por balancear nuestros demonios internos y hacer que coexita con el orden y desorden.
		Play as an affirmation of humanity occurs because we have to strive to balance it—to tie our demons and make them coexist with our passion for order39 without falling in the mindless focus that lures us toward structured play.

(Tema::Sobre la gamificación (21))
		There is a certain pleasure in rational, goal-oriented play. While instrumental play can be a highly positive type of play (Taylor 2006a), it can also lead to worse instances of instrumental play (Sicart 2012), in which the very purpose of play is lost in external rewards and mindless interactions.

(Tema::Bahtkin y el carnaval (21))
	Carnaval para exteriorizar.
		The carnival, as Russian philosopher Mikhail Bakhtin described it, is an outcome of the expressive capacity of play,43 managing the careful relations between creation and destruction.
	(22) Carnaval y liberación
		Laughter liberates not only from external censorship but first of all from the great interior censor; it liberates from the fear that developed in man during thousands of years: fear of the sacred, of prohibitions, of the past, of power.

(Tema::El juego es apropiativo (22))
		Play is appropriative, in that it takes over the context in which it exists and cannot be totally predetermined by such context. From the context of use of a toy to a game, from a ritual to a playground, context becomes servant to the activity of playing.

(Tema::Sobre el espectador (25))
	El espectador también se apropia de las cosas, aunque en menor medida
		To see a game being played, a sport or something like Ninja or Joust, is also to participate, to play—a minor, perhaps secondary way, but also a way of performing the basic appropriative move that defines play as an activity.

(Tema::El juego es disruptivo (26))
		Play is disruptive as a consequence of being appropriate.
	(26) El juego es disruptivo porque podemos ir más allá de las convenciones.
		But like all other passing pleasures, play can also disruptively reveal our conventions, assumptions, biases, and dislikes. In disrupting the normal state of affairs by being playful, we can go beyond fun when we appropriate a context with the intention of playing with and within it. And in that move, we reveal the inner workings of the context that we inhabit. An interesting example of the potential disruptiveness of play is the activist performance Camover.56 In Camover, players are encouraged to destroy CCTV cameras in a specific urban environment and are awarded points for doing so—the points are made available and visible on a website. This political (and illegal) action uses gamelike elements, such as points or the creation of a shared play community that evaluates the players’ performance, to communicate a political message.
		

(Tema::El juego es autotélico (28))
	El juego es autotélico, tiene su propio fin. Se negocia contínuamente.
		Play is autotelic in its context, but it is also negotiated. Its autotelic nature is always being discussed and negotiated. We play by negotiating the purposes of play, how far we want to extend the influences of the play activity, and how much we play for the purpose of playing or for the purpose of personal expression.

(Tema::El juego es creativo(29))
	El juego como forma de expresión
		Play is creative, in that it affords players different degrees of expression inherent in the play activity itself. Playing is both accepting the rules of the game and performing within them according to our needs, personality, and constitution of a playing community. Play is the act of creatively engaging with the world, with technologies, contexts, and objects, from games to toys and playgrounds, exploring them through ludic interaction.

(Tema::El juego es personal (29))
	El juego es personal. Jugamos según nuestra moral y política, quienes somos se ve reflejado en el proceso creativo del juego
		Finally, play is personal. Even when we play with others, the effects of play are individual, attached to our own sentimental, moral, and political memories. Who we are is also who plays, the kind of person we let lose when we play. Our memories are composed of these instances of play, the victories and defeats, but also the shared moments.

#### 02. Playfulness

En esta sección se discute el concepto de "jugueteo" o "playfulness", que se refiere a una actitud lúdica en la vida cotidiana, incluyendo el trabajo y otras actividades. Este enfoque va más allá de los límites tradicionales del juego, abordando actividades serias como si fueran juegos. Aunque no es autotélico, se orienta a la actividad y es disruptivo, ya que rompe con la normalidad del contexto. Esta actitud es apropiativa, personal, expresiva y creativa, transformando espacios y actividades en experiencias lúdicas. Además, tiene un aspecto carnivalesco al subvertir las reglas de la situación original. Es fundamental entender que se trata de una actitud más que del juego en sí.

(Tema::Crítica hacia la gamificación (126))
	Un playfulness orientado hacia lo comercial. 
		The rise of gamification as a concept in 2010 is testimony to this idea—that through play and its values, businesses and services can better engage consumers. Gamification in its commercial phrasing was widely criticized, yet there is still some hope in thinking about playfulness outside the domain of formalized play. See Deterding et al. (2011a, 2011b) for a complete, thorough, and hopeful critique of the gamification.

(Tema::Playfulness (33))
	Capacidad de jugar fuera del contexto del juego. Es una forma, una actitud, de estar con un contexto o los objetos. Similar al juego, pero respetando el propósito o los objetivos del objeto en su contexto.
	- Es una fisica, psicologica y actitud emocional hacia las cosas, personas y situaciones.
	(37) Con el mundo
	Junta una ecología de las cosas jugables, situaciones, comportamientos y personas, extendiendo el juego hacia una actitud de ser en el mundo.
	(38-39) elementos a considerad
	Playfulness no es autotélico porque no es una actividad; es apropiativo porque se apropia de un contexto que no está hecho para jugar; es disruptivo porque revela comportaminetos, tecnologías o situaciones que no estaban para jugar; es personal, es expresivo
	(42) Resistencia
	Muchos objetos o lugares están diseñados para resistirse al playfulness. De igual forma hay muchos otros que son diseñados para serlo, pero no es su totalidad. 
	(43) diseño
	El diseño del playfulness es ambigup, autosuficiente. Pone sobre la mesa un diseño oriantado hacia el usuario, diseñador, contexto y propósito.
	(46) Importancia
	Extender hacia afuera la importancia de jugar más allá de los límites autotélicos.
